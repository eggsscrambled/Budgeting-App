About Us

Hey thanks for using Better Budget!

My name is Cooper and I'm a college student who didn't start budgetting until I had to in order to manage my finances during school. Over the last year I kept track of all my icome and spending by hand and it changed the way I see money for the better; and I've been able to save much more and do things I wasn't able to before! My goal with Better Budget is to provide a fast and easy way for young people to gain the same insight that changed my money situation for the better at an age where it can feel impossible to manage so many new things.

I had wanted to start budgetting before I started college but it felt like there was a certain way to do it and I didn't know how so I never tried. When I was forced to start I realized there is no "right" way to budget, only a way that works for you, and that ANY kind of budget is better than NO budget.

I hope Better Budget can either help you start managing your money and give you some newfound freedom, or speed up your current budgetting process so you dont spend hours doing all the math with a calculator and a spread sheet (like I did)

Thank you for using Better Budget, and if youd like to support me and my journey in computer science so I can build more things like this please do with the links below!
